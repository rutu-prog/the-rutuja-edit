About me

The mind behind the marketing.

Brands have stories. I love finding ways to tell them.

I’m Rutuja, an MBA student at IIM Bodh Gaya (2025–27) and a marketing and branding enthusiast. I’m happiest where strategy meets creativity: working out what a brand stands for, what makes its audience care, and how a good idea turns into a campaign people remember.

Tech Mahindra gave me my closest look at brand work from the inside. This year I benchmarked its digital presence against Accenture, IBM and KPMG, read social and Sprinklr data for insights, and worked on campaign ideas around “Scale at Speed”.

Before that I managed social media at The Punch Loft, supported hiring at EY, and led a team of 90 students as Joint Secretary at Ruparel, where I also wrote and curated content as Literary Arts Head. At IIM Bodh Gaya I’m a member of E-Cell, where I’ve mobilised ₹10 lakh+ in sponsorships and supported TEDx IIM Bodh Gaya.

What I want next is a career in brand management and product management, alongside marketing, advertising and creative communications. I’m still learning, and I’d love to keep doing it somewhere that turns ideas into campaigns and products.
